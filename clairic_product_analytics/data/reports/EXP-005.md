# EXP-005 · Lower free-shipping threshold

Can we close the shipping-threshold question? EXP-005 ran 2026-02-22–2026-03-07, inclusive UTC, for UK and Ireland visitors on mobile. A had 48,761 eligible visitors and 1,436 purchasers; B had 48,836 and 1,421. Merchandise revenue was GBP 74,813.70 and GBP 71,214.35, respectively.

Lowering the threshold may remove a checkout objection, but the shipping subsidy still needs to be paid. Margin moved from 45.54% to 41.74%. Pause the launch until we have resolved the commercial guardrail.
